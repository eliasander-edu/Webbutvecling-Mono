Internet

[ˈɪn.tə.net]

noun (the Internet)

a global computer network providing a variety of information and communication facitlities, consisting of interconnected networks using standardized communication protocols: the guide is also available on the Internet | [as modifier] : Internet access | an Internet address.

Origin

1970s (denoting a computer network connection two or more smaller networks): from inter- 'reciprocal, mutual' + network
